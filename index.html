How I work

Fast,
unlit,
close

Festival light changes every eight seconds and nobody is going to wait for you. I shoot fast, I don't add light, and I get close enough that the frame feels like the room felt.

Pit access, backstage, crowd, site — I cover the whole day, not just the headliner. Galleries turn around quickly because a festival photo is worth the most the morning after.
